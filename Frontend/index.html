Where "Embo" comes from

Five barrios, one root word.

  • CemboCentral Enlisted Men's Barrio — the first of the five, settled in 1949 by servicemen of the Philippine Ground Force.
  • ComemboCombat Enlisted Men's Barrio — home to the Philippine Army's Combat Engineering Group from 1957.
  • PemboPanthers Enlisted Men's Barrio — named for the First Ranger Regiment, the "Panthers."
  • West & East RemboRiverside Enlisted Men's Barrio — settled along the Pasig-Pateros riverside.
  • All five trace back to Fort Bonifacio's enlisted personnel housing. Different units, one shared "EMBO" — and today, one shared reservation system.
