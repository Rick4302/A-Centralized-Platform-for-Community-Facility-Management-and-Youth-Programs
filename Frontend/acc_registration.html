Terms of Use

Welcome to EMBO Access. By registering for an account, you agree to the following terms:

  • Residency Verification: You confirm that you are a bona fide resident of Cembo, West Rembo, East Rembo, Comembo, or Pembo.
  • Account Integrity: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all activities under your account.
  • Fair Usage: Facility bookings, court reservations, and event sign-ups must be made in good faith. Fraudulent or abusive bookings will lead to account suspension.

Privacy Notice

EMBO Access values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

  • Data Collected: We collect your name, barangay address, contact number, and email address solely for identity verification and facility reservation management.
  • Data Protection: Your information is stored securely in Firebase Firestore with strict access control rules.
  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties outside of your barangay administration offices.
